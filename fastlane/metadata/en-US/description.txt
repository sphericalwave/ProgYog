groundWerk is a structured strength and mobility trainer for yoga and bodyweight skills — handstands, splits, arm balances, and the progressions that get you there.

REAL-TIME FORM COACHING
Point your camera at yourself and groundWerk overlays a live skeleton tracking your alignment as you move, powered by on-device Apple Vision — no footage leaves your phone.

STRUCTURED SKILL PROGRESSIONS
Browse skill families and progressive series, each broken into ordered stages so you always know the next achievable step — not just the end goal.

GUIDED SESSIONS, LOGGED SETS
Run a session, log each set, and get a completion score when you're done. Every rep is recorded so your history is never just a memory.

HEART RATE + PROGRESS DASHBOARDS
Pair a BLE chest strap to capture live heart rate during practice. Swift Charts dashboards track HR trends, per-skill progress, and completion across every family over time.

APPLE HEALTH + CALENDAR
Finished sessions log to Health as Yoga workouts and mirror into a dedicated calendar, deep-linking back into the app from either place.

REMINDERS, BUILT IN
Local notifications keep your next session on schedule — no separate reminders app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data is stored in Core Data and synced across your devices with iCloud — never a third-party server.

Whether you're working toward your first handstand or maintaining years of mobility work, groundWerk gives you the structure, feedback, and history to actually see the progress.

# App Store metadata — groundWerk

Source of truth for App Store Connect listing copy. Update here first, then mirror any
changes into `fastlane/metadata/en-US/*.txt` (deliver reads from there, not this file).

## Pushing to App Store Connect

```
export ASC_KEY_ID=...
export ASC_ISSUER_ID=...
export ASC_KEY_PATH=/path/to/AuthKey_XXXX.p8   # App Store Connect API key
bundle exec fastlane deliver_metadata
```

`deliver_metadata` (see `fastlane/Fastfile`) pushes description/keywords/promotional text
from `fastlane/metadata/en-US/` and screenshots from `fastlane/screenshots/en-US/` —
`skip_binary_upload` and `submit_for_review: false` keep it metadata-only, no build upload,
no review submission. `deliver` shows a diff and asks for confirmation before pushing
unless run with `--force`.

## Description

(4000 char max)

```
groundWerk is a structured strength and mobility trainer for yoga and bodyweight skills —
handstands, splits, arm balances, and the progressions that get you there.

REAL-TIME FORM COACHING
Point your camera at yourself and groundWerk overlays a live skeleton tracking your
alignment as you move, powered by on-device Apple Vision — no footage leaves your phone.

STRUCTURED SKILL PROGRESSIONS
Browse skill families and progressive series, each broken into ordered stages so you
always know the next achievable step — not just the end goal.

GUIDED SESSIONS, LOGGED SETS
Run a session, log each set, and get a completion score when you're done. Every rep is
recorded so your history is never just a memory.

HEART RATE + PROGRESS DASHBOARDS
Pair a BLE chest strap to capture live heart rate during practice. Swift Charts dashboards
track HR trends, per-skill progress, and completion across every family over time.

APPLE HEALTH + CALENDAR
Finished sessions log to Health as Yoga workouts and mirror into a dedicated calendar,
deep-linking back into the app from either place.

REMINDERS, BUILT IN
Local notifications keep your next session on schedule — no separate reminders app.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data is stored in Core Data and synced across your devices with iCloud — never a
third-party server.

Whether you're working toward your first handstand or maintaining years of mobility work,
groundWerk gives you the structure, feedback, and history to actually see the progress.
```
